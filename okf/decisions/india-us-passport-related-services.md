# Indian Passport Related Services Router — USA

These services may use passport information and Passport Seva/VFS infrastructure, but they are **not Ordinary Passport issuance or re-issue**.

## `police_clearance_certificate`

Use for an Indian passport holder seeking PCC for an eligible purpose such as residence status, employment, long-term visa or immigration. Current VFS guidance says PCC is not issued for travel on a tourist visa.

Route to [PCC requirements](../requirements/india-us-pcc-documents.md).

## `global_entry_background_verification`

Use only after the applicant has initiated U.S. CBP Global Entry and needs the Government of India background-verification portion.

Route to [GEP requirements](../requirements/india-us-gep-documents.md).

## `surrender_indian_passport`

Use after acquisition of foreign nationality. A former Indian citizen must not continue using an Indian passport and must not be routed to Ordinary Passport re-issue.

Route to [Surrender requirements](../requirements/india-us-passport-surrender-documents.md).

## Separation guardrail

PCC, GEP and surrender must each use their own application, fee and checklist. The presence of a passport number does not make them re-issue cases.
