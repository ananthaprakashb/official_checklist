# U.S. PERM Stage Classification

Classify the employer's actual PERM stage before producing a checklist.

## Standard PERM stages

- `planning`
- `prevailing_wage`
- `recruitment`
- `eta9089_ready`
- `eta9089_filed`
- `audit`
- `supervised_recruitment`
- `certified`
- `denied`
- `reconsideration_or_balca`

## Occupation-route gate

This detailed workflow is for the standard 20 CFR 656.17 professional/nonprofessional PERM route.

Route the following out for separate treatment rather than pretending the standard recruitment calendar applies unchanged:

- college/university teacher special handling under 20 CFR 656.18;
- professional athlete cases;
- Schedule A cases, which do not use ordinary DOL PERM processing.

## Classification-first guardrail

Do not ask an employer to assemble ETA-9089 evidence until the occupation route, PWD status, and applicable recruitment method are resolved.
