# Indian Passport Adverse Action and Appeal Route

Use this route only after an adverse passport decision/action has occurred.

## Covered adverse actions

- passport application rejection;
- passport refusal;
- passport impounding;
- passport revocation.

## Online appeal route

When the affected application/file belongs to a live Passport Seva location, the official flow allows the applicant to log an appeal in Passport Seva, identify the affected application/file, enter appeal details, pay the appeal fee and upload supporting material.

Core evidence includes the official notification/speaking order or rejection/refusal/impounding/revocation notice and other relevant supporting documents.

## Offline exception

Passport Seva states that an offline appeal route is used for a file belonging to a non-live Mission/Post/other location. This must be resolved from the actual file/location rather than assumed.

## Guardrail

Do not tell an applicant to submit a new Fresh or Re-issue application merely to bypass an existing adverse action. Resolve whether appeal, compliance with a deficiency notice, or a new application is the correct authoritative route first.
