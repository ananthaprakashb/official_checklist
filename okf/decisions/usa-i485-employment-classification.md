# Employment-Based I-485 Classification

Resolve before documents:

1. exact employment-based immigrant basis / Visa Bulletin row;
2. country of chargeability;
3. priority date;
4. filing month;
5. the filing chart USCIS selected for that month;
6. underlying petition posture;
7. whether the applicant is physically present in the United States and otherwise using adjustment rather than consular processing.

## Guardrail

A favorable Department of State Dates-for-Filing cutoff does not authorize an I-485 filing unless USCIS selected that chart for employment-based adjustment filings for the month. Final Action availability is always evaluated separately because approval cannot occur without an available immigrant visa number.
