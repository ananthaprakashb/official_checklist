# Employment-Based NVC / DS-260 Classification

Classify the immigrant basis before collecting NVC fees or documents.

Supported I-140-based consular routes:

- EB-1A / E11 — extraordinary ability; no permanent job-offer requirement.
- EB-1B / E12 — outstanding professor/researcher; employer/job-offer based.
- EB-1C / E13 — multinational manager/executive; employer/job-offer based.
- EB-2 regular / E21 — employer/job-offer based.
- EB-2 NIW / E21 NIW — no permanent job-offer requirement.
- EB-3 Skilled Worker / E31 — employer/job-offer based.
- EB-3 Professional / E32 — employer/job-offer based.
- EB-3 Other Worker / EW3 — employer/job-offer based.
- Schedule A mapped to its underlying EB-2 or EB-3 preference — employer/job-offer based.

EB-4 and EB-5 use different petition/classification families and must not inherit the I-140-based NVC evidence model merely because both eventually use immigrant-visa processing.

An approved I-140 alone does not prove that NVC has the case. Confirm the USCIS/DOS routing before entering CEAC/NVC steps.
