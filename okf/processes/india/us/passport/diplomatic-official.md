# Diplomatic / Official Passport

Diplomatic and Official passports are separate passport categories with category-specific entitlement and institutional routing. They are not ordinary-passport upgrades.

## Route

1. Resolve requested document category: `diplomatic_passport` or `official_passport`.
2. Confirm entitlement/official sponsorship through the competent Government authority.
3. Use the Passport Seva Diplomatic/Official Passport workflow and designated submission channel.
4. Do not send the applicant through the ordinary VFS Fresh/Re-issue checklist unless an authoritative source explicitly requires that channel for the case.

## Requirements

- [Special passport/document requirements](../../../../requirements/india-us-passport-special-documents.md)

Because entitlement and sponsoring-authority documents are case-specific, unresolved cases must return `NEEDS_AUTHORITATIVE_CONFIRMATION`.
