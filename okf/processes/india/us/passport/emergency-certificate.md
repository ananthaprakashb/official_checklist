# Emergency Certificate — USA

An Emergency Certificate (EC) is a distinct emergency travel-document path. It must not be represented as an expedited passport Re-issue.

## Route

1. Confirm the applicant needs the EC branch rather than a passport under Regular/Tatkaal processing.
2. Confirm the purpose and eligibility with the responsible Indian Mission.
3. Use the Mission-specific EC application/document route.
4. Treat EC as a one-way/emergency travel-document workflow when the official Mission guidance says so.

## Requirements and fee

- [Emergency Certificate documents](../../../../requirements/india-us-emergency-certificate-documents.md)
- [Emergency Certificate fee](../../../../fees/india-us-emergency-certificate-fee.md)

## Submission

EC is Mission-controlled. Do not assume the VFS ordinary-passport submission path applies. See [submission routing](../../../../submission/india-us-passport-submission.md).

## Final gate

If the applicant actually needs a normal passport usable for continuing international travel, do not mark an EC route `READY` solely because it is faster.
