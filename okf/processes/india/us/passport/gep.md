# Global Entry Program Background Verification — USA

This is the Indian background-verification component associated with Global Entry Program participation. It is not a passport Fresh/Re-issue application.

## Route

1. Confirm the requested outcome is GEP background verification.
2. Follow the Government of India GEP service/application route.
3. Resolve the Indian Mission/jurisdiction where applicable.
4. Use the current official conditional document requirements; do not substitute PCC or passport-issuance documents unless an official GEP source explicitly requires them.

## Requirements

- [GEP requirements](../../../../requirements/india-us-gep-documents.md)

## Final gate

If the applicant actually needs PCC for immigration/employment rather than GEP verification, reclassify before payment or submission.
