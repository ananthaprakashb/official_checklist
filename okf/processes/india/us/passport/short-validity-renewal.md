# Renewal of Short Validity Passport — USA

Passport Seva exposes **Renewal of Short Validity Passport (SVP)** as its own Re-issue case. Do not collapse it into ordinary expiry without checking the reason the earlier passport received short validity.

## Route

1. Classify as Ordinary Passport → Re-issue.
2. Select `renewal_short_validity_passport` in the Re-issue reason set.
3. Preserve the original short-validity reason and any authority-specific follow-up requirements.
4. If the current official checklist does not resolve the SVP-specific evidence for the applicant's facts, return `NEEDS_AUTHORITATIVE_CONFIRMATION` rather than borrowing an ordinary-expiry checklist.
