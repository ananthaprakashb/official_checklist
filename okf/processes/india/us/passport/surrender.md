# Surrender of Indian Passport — USA

Surrender is a distinct post-citizenship-change service. It must not be routed as passport Re-issue, even when the physical Indian passport is still valid.

## Route

1. Confirm the applicant's citizenship/nationality facts require surrender under the current official rules.
2. Select the Surrender service rather than Fresh/Re-issue/PCC.
3. Resolve whether the passport is available, lost, or otherwise unavailable because the supporting-document branch can differ.
4. Use the VFS/Passport Seva surrender workflow applicable to the applicant's facts.

## Requirements

- [Surrender documents](../../../../requirements/india-us-passport-surrender-documents.md)

## Final gate

If the applicant is still eligible to hold/use an Indian passport and has not triggered the surrender service, do not direct them into this branch.
