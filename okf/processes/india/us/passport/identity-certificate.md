# Identity Certificate

Identity Certificate is a distinct Indian travel-document category for eligible applicants. It must not be classified as an ordinary Fresh passport merely because the applicant does not hold a passport.

## Route

1. Determine whether the applicant is legally eligible for Identity Certificate rather than an ordinary Indian passport.
2. Resolve the competent issuing authority and current jurisdiction-specific workflow.
3. Collect only the category-specific evidence requested by the authoritative current source.

## Guardrail

The present U.S. bundle verifies the existence of this document family but does not yet encode a universal U.S. applicant checklist. Until a current Mission-specific checklist resolves the case, return `NEEDS_AUTHORITATIVE_CONFIRMATION`.
