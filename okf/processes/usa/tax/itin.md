# IRS Individual Taxpayer Identification Number (ITIN)

Resolve eligibility and submission method before mailing identity documents.

Primary branches:

1. new ITIN;
2. ITIN renewal;
3. response to CP566/CP567 or another ITIN notice;
4. delayed-application status inquiry.

For a new/renewal W-7 package, confirm that the applicant is not eligible for an SSN, determine whether a U.S. federal tax return or documented W-7 exception applies, prepare current identity/foreign-status evidence, and choose a mail/TAC/VITA/CAA/AA submission route.

A `READY` result means the package route is resolved; the IRS still determines whether the W-7 and claimed exception/evidence are acceptable.
