# U.S. Passport Services

Classify the requested outcome before choosing a State Department form or appointment channel.

Primary branches:

1. first adult passport — in-person DS-11 route;
2. child under 16 — in-person DS-11 with parental/guardian rules;
3. eligible renewal — current online or mail renewal channel;
4. lost/stolen or damaged passport — replacement rather than ordinary renewal;
5. name/data correction — resolve the correct correction form based on the circumstances;
6. limited-validity replacement — use limitation-specific instructions;
7. urgent travel — passport agency/center only when the current travel-window criteria are met.

A `READY` result means the procedural branch is resolved; it does not predict passport issuance.
