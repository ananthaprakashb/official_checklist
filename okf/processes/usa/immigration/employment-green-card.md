# Employment-Based Green Card

## Stage router

1. `perm` — determine whether the selected immigrant category requires DOL permanent labor certification; if yes, complete the employer-side PERM process before I-140.
2. `i140` — file the correct I-140 petition type with labor certification when required and select the intended adjustment/consular destination.
3. `i485` — before filing, confirm the applicant is eligible to adjust in the United States and confirm current visa availability using both the Department of State Visa Bulletin and USCIS monthly chart selection.
4. `consular` — after petition approval and transfer, follow NVC/CEAC/DS-260 and immigrant-visa interview processing.

Do not treat these as interchangeable forms. A category that does not require PERM must not be forced through PERM, and an approved I-140 must not be treated as automatic authorization to file I-485.
