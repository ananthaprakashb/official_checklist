# Family-Based Green Card

Classify the petitioner status and relationship before assuming visa availability or preparing adjustment/consular documents.

Core classification:

- U.S. citizen spouse, unmarried child under 21, and qualifying parent are immediate-relative categories with immigrant visas not subject to family-preference numerical limits;
- U.S. citizen adult unmarried sons/daughters, married sons/daughters, and siblings fall into family-preference categories;
- lawful permanent residents may petition for spouses/unmarried children and unmarried adult sons/daughters in the applicable F2 categories, but not ordinary I-130 parent/sibling/married-child categories;
- a U.S. citizen must be at least 21 to petition for a parent or sibling.

After I-130 classification, resolve adjustment of status inside the United States versus NVC/immigrant-visa processing abroad. An approved I-130 does not itself grant permanent residence, work authorization, or permission to remain in the United States. Family-preference cases must use the current Visa Bulletin and applicable USCIS filing-chart rules before an I-485 or immigrant-visa step is treated as available.
