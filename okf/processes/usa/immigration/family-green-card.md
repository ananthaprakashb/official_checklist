# Family-Based Green Card

## Stage router

1. Establish the qualifying petitioner/beneficiary relationship and correct I-130 family category.
2. Determine whether the category is an immediate-relative or family-preference route and whether a visa-number gate applies.
3. If the beneficiary is in the United States and independently eligible to adjust, evaluate the I-485 route and any permitted concurrent filing.
4. If the beneficiary will process abroad, follow petition approval → NVC/CEAC → fees/support documents → DS-260/civil documents → medical/interview.

An approved I-130 establishes a petition stage; it does not itself grant lawful permanent residence.
