# U.S. PERM Detailed Preflight

Standard PERM path:

`job opportunity → ETA-9141/PWD → prefiling recruitment + Notice of Filing → ETA-9089 → analyst review → audit and/or supervised recruitment when ordered → certification or denial/review → I-140 handoff`

## Pre-filing gates

1. Confirm this is a standard professional or nonprofessional PERM case under 20 CFR 656.17.
2. Confirm the employer has a valid prevailing wage determination for the actual job opportunity.
3. Confirm recruitment began or ETA-9089 was filed within the PWD validity period.
4. Confirm the SWA job order, newspaper/professional-journal advertising, professional additional recruitment (when applicable), Notice of Filing, layoff duties, and recruitment report are complete.
5. Confirm each dated recruitment step falls inside its regulatory filing window.
6. Confirm ETA-9089 reflects the actual minimum requirements, worksite(s), wage, recruitment and foreign-worker facts without using a simpler but incorrect branch.

## Post-filing gates

- `audit`: answer the exact audit letter by its deadline; preserve the one-extension rule rather than assuming an extension exists.
- `supervised_recruitment`: follow the Certifying Officer's instructions, obtain advertisement approval before publication, and meet each CO-directed response deadline.
- `certified`: preserve the ETA-9089 filing date as the priority-date basis and file the certification with the correct I-140 petition within the 180-day validity period.
- `denied`: evaluate reconsideration/BALCA review from the actual Final Determination and 30-day deadline.

A result of `READY` means the selected PERM stage and objective timing checks pass this checklist; it does not predict DOL or USCIS adjudication.
