# Employment-Based NVC / DS-260 Consular Processing

This process is the post-petition consular branch for supported employment-based immigrant cases.

Sequence:

1. Confirm the exact employment-based classification and approved immigrant petition.
2. Confirm USCIS has forwarded the approved petition to DOS/NVC; resolve a retained/non-forwarded petition before treating NVC as active.
3. Preserve the priority date and country of chargeability.
4. Use current Department of State Visa Bulletin data and the actual NVC notice to determine whether NVC pre-processing can advance.
5. Receive the NVC Welcome Letter / CEAC credentials.
6. Pay the required immigrant-visa fee for each intending immigrant and the Affidavit-of-Support fee only when applicable.
7. Complete DS-260 for every intending immigrant.
8. Submit civil/employment/financial-support evidence following CEAC/NVC instructions and country reciprocity rules.
9. Obtain NVC's documentarily complete determination.
10. Wait for both Final Action visa availability and post interview capacity.
11. Follow the appointment letter, post-specific instructions, medical and interview document requirements.
12. At interview, follow any INA 221(g)/administrative-processing instructions exactly.
13. After visa issuance, pay the USCIS Immigrant Fee unless exempt and enter the United States before visa expiration.
14. CBP admission on the immigrant visa establishes permanent-resident admission; Green Card production follows USCIS processing.
