# Employment-Based Form I-485 Detailed Preflight

`employment basis → priority date → USCIS monthly filing chart → physical presence/admission/status gate → I-485 package → I-693/Supplement J → I-765/I-131 → pending-case events → 180-day portability → Final Action availability → decision`

READY means the objective filing or pending-case gates encoded here are satisfied. It does not predict discretionary adjustment approval, admissibility, visa allocation, or USCIS adjudication.
