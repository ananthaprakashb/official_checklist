# U.S. Visa Application Router

## Nonimmigrant visa

Use the DS-160 route for nonimmigrant visa applications and then follow the specific Embassy/Consulate instructions for fee, interview-waiver eligibility and appointment scheduling. Petition-based workers must align DS-160 data with the underlying petition/receipt.

## Immigrant visa

Use NVC/CEAC/DS-260 only after the immigrant petition reaches the consular-processing stage and NVC instructs the applicant to proceed. Collect civil documents, financial-support documents where applicable, medical examination and interview originals under the current NVC/post instructions.

Never interchange DS-160 and DS-260 solely because both are Department of State online forms.
