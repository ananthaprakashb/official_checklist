# Permanent Resident and Citizenship Services

## Card maintenance

Use I-90 for qualifying ten-year card renewal/replacement/correction. Do not use I-90 to remove two-year conditions; marriage-based conditional residents generally use I-751 and conditional immigrant investors use I-829.

## Travel documents

Classify the requested I-131 document before filing: reentry permit, refugee travel document, advance parole or another authorized travel-document branch.

## Naturalization

Resolve the N-400 eligibility basis before filing. If facts indicate the person may already have acquired or derived U.S. citizenship, resolve citizenship documentation rather than blindly submitting N-400.

## Address change

Use the current USCIS change-of-address route and separately update DOS/NVC or sponsorship records when those independent duties apply.
