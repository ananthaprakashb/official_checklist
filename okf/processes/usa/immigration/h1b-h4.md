# H-1B / H-4 / H-4 EAD

- H-1B employer petition: Form I-129 plus the classification-specific H-1B evidence and DOL LCA; cap-subject cases must align with the applicable selected registration when required.
- H-4 extension/change of status inside the United States: evaluate Form I-539 eligibility and timing.
- H-4 visa issuance abroad: use the Department of State nonimmigrant visa/DS-160 route rather than I-539 for visa issuance.
- H-4 EAD: use Form I-765 category (c)(26) only when the H-4 spouse meets the qualifying H-1B principal basis, including approved I-140 or qualifying AC21 extension.

These branches share family/status facts but are not the same application.
