# U.S. Immigration & Visa Services

Use [service classification](../../../decisions/usa-immigration-service-classification.md) before choosing forms or collecting evidence.

The classifier covers employment/family permanent residence, adjustment versus consular processing, temporary visa applications, H-1B/H-4, EAD, travel documents, permanent-resident card maintenance, naturalization, address changes and I-94 issues.

The classifier identifies the correct service family and agency handoff. It is not a substitute for individualized legal advice or a final eligibility determination.
