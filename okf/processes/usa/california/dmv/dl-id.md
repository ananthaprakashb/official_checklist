# California DMV Driver License, ID and REAL ID

Classify the DMV transaction before collecting documents or scheduling an office visit.

## Service families

1. REAL ID upgrade or REAL ID selection during a DL/ID transaction.
2. First adult California driver license.
3. Under-18 instruction permit and provisional license.
4. Transfer of a valid driver license from another U.S. state.
5. Transfer of a foreign-country driver license.
6. Driver-license renewal.
7. Driver-license replacement.
8. First, renewal, or replacement California identification card.
9. Legal name / DL-ID information update.
10. Address change.
11. AB 60 driver license.

## Guardrails

- Do not treat a federal-noncompliant California DL/ID as REAL ID.
- Do not route an AB 60 applicant into REAL ID.
- Do not assume a foreign-country license waives the California behind-the-wheel test.
- Do not treat a teen provisional-license application as the same transaction as the initial instruction permit.
- Do not send a legal-name-change applicant to DMV before the required SSA update is complete.
- Do not assume every renewal requires a knowledge test; use the current DMV renewal notice and current DMV instructions.
- Do not treat an address change as automatic issuance of a replacement physical DL/ID.

`READY` means the correct procedural branch and known prerequisites are aligned. It does not predict DMV approval or waive DMV document verification, vision/testing, or safety determinations.
