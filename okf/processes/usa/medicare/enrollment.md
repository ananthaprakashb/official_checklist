# Medicare Enrollment & Part B Timing

Classify the enrollment period before submitting Part A/Part B forms.

Primary branches:

1. Initial Enrollment Period around age 65;
2. add Part B after Part A is already active;
3. current-employment group-coverage Special Enrollment Period;
4. exceptional-condition Special Enrollment Period;
5. General Enrollment Period;
6. Medicare Advantage / Part D plan-change event.

The workflow distinguishes current-employment group health coverage from COBRA/retiree coverage and surfaces the standard 8-month Part B SEP window. Late-enrollment penalties and unusual eligibility circumstances require current Medicare/SSA confirmation.
