# Passport Seva Surrender Certificate Guidance

Authoritative general guidance for Surrender Certificate.

Key facts used by this bundle:

- application is initiated through Passport Seva;
- original passport to be surrendered and proof of foreign nationality are core documents;
- police verification is not required for Surrender Certificate;
- an expired latest passport can be surrendered;
- surrender is not allowed for an impounded, revoked, suspended or cancelled-against-reissue passport through the ordinary surrender path.

The U.S. submission path and current U.S. checklist are governed by VFS/Mission guidance.
