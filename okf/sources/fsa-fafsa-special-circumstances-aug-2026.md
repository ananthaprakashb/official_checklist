# FAFSA dependency and special-circumstance routing

Key controls:

- FAFSA dependency status is determined under federal student-aid law and is not the same as IRS tax dependency, residence with parents, or whether parents financially support the student;
- unusual circumstances that prevent parent contact or make contact unsafe can allow a provisionally independent FAFSA submission without parent information; the school's financial aid office must resolve the dependency determination/documentation;
- a dependent student whose parents merely refuse to participate uses a different branch: the student may request consideration for Direct Unsubsidized Loan only and is not eligible through that branch for Pell Grant or most other federal aid;
- a significant change in financial circumstances after the tax year is generally handled with the school's financial aid office through professional judgment after the FAFSA is submitted, not by substituting a different tax year in the FAFSA.
