# USCIS Immigrant Fee

Current USCIS controls:

- Most people immigrating with an immigrant visa must pay the USCIS Immigrant Fee unless an exemption applies.
- USCIS strongly encourages payment after the immigrant visa is received and before departure for the United States.
- Payment may occur after U.S. admission, but USCIS will not produce the Permanent Resident Card until the fee is paid.
- The fee itself is not treated by this preflight as a substitute for a valid immigrant visa or CBP admission.
