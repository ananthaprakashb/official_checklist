# IRS ITIN — August 2026

Authoritative routing source for applying for or renewing an Individual Taxpayer Identification Number.

Key controls:

- an ITIN is for a person who needs a U.S. taxpayer identification number but is not eligible for an SSN;
- Form W-7 is generally filed with a U.S. federal tax return unless a documented IRS exception applies;
- supporting evidence must prove identity and foreign status and, for certain dependents, U.S. residency;
- current supporting documents must be originals or issuing-agency-certified copies when required;
- IRS supports mail, qualifying Taxpayer Assistance Center/VITA services, Certifying Acceptance Agents and Acceptance Agents with different document-authentication capabilities.
