# USCIS Form I-140 Instructions — August 2026

Current authoritative instructions for Form I-140, edition 06/07/24.

Controls used by Official Checklist:

- Form I-140 covers EB-1 extraordinary ability, outstanding professor/researcher, multinational executive/manager; EB-2 advanced-degree/exceptional-ability and NIW; and EB-3 skilled worker, professional and other worker classifications.
- Only one petition type is selected for a filing.
- EB-1A may be filed by the beneficiary or another petitioner and does not require a labor certification or job offer, but the beneficiary must intend to continue work in the area of expertise.
- EB-1B and EB-1C require a qualifying U.S. employer and category-specific evidence.
- Regular EB-2 and EB-3 employer petitions require the labor-certification route applicable to the case and evidence that the beneficiary satisfies the classification and job requirements.
- NIW may waive the job-offer and labor-certification requirements but still requires the beneficiary to qualify for EB-2 and independently satisfy the NIW standard.
- Schedule A cases are filed directly with USCIS using the Schedule A labor-certification procedure rather than ordinary DOL-certified PERM.
