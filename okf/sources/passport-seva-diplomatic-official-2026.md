# Passport Seva Diplomatic and Official Passport Guidance

Authoritative source for Diplomatic and Official passport classification and core documentation.

Passport Seva states that these passports are issued to people holding diplomatic status or deputed by the Government of India for official duty abroad. The document advisor identifies official ID, Head of Office certificate, forwarding-officer request and applicable political/PMO clearance, together with existing passport/safe-custody evidence where relevant.

The U.S. OKF bundle does not reuse the ordinary-passport VFS checklist for this category; mission-specific handling must be confirmed before a READY result.
