# PERM Prevailing Wage Determination — August 2026

Key controls used by Official Checklist:

- A PERM application requires a prevailing wage determination issued by the National Prevailing Wage Center for the job opportunity.
- Employers request the determination using Form ETA-9141.
- The PWD carries a validity period. Under 20 CFR 656.40(c), the validity period may not be less than 90 days or more than one year from the determination date.
- To use the PWD for PERM, the employer must either file the PERM application or begin the required recruitment during the PWD validity period.
- The offered wage may not be lower than the applicable prevailing wage.
