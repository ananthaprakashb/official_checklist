# Employment-Based Immigrant Visas

Current Department of State controls used by this bundle:

- After USCIS approves an employment-based immigrant petition designated for consular processing, the petition is generally transferred to the National Visa Center (NVC).
- NVC assigns a case number and performs pre-processing for preference cases when the priority date reaches the applicable processing range.
- Each intending immigrant, including qualified derivatives, pays the required immigrant-visa fee and completes the immigrant-visa application when instructed.
- Employment-based applicants must preserve the qualifying petition/classification through visa adjudication; employer-sponsored categories require the qualifying employment basis to remain available.
- A spouse and unmarried child under 21 may qualify as derivative applicants subject to their own eligibility, application, fee, civil-document and medical requirements.
- Employment-based cases do not universally require Form I-864. The Department identifies a limited Affidavit-of-Support rule when a U.S. citizen or lawful permanent resident relative filed the employment petition or has a significant ownership interest in the petitioning entity.
