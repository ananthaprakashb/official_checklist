# Revised U.S. Consular Jurisdictions — 2026

Current CGI San Francisco guidance states that, effective 1 August 2025, the Consulate General of India in Los Angeles has jurisdiction over Southern California's listed 10 counties, Arizona, Nevada and New Mexico.

The same current official page also states that **CGI San Francisco will continue to provide consular services for the jurisdiction currently under CGI Los Angeles until further notice**.

This transition explains why the current VFS U.S. passport page continues to route those areas through the San Francisco VFS application centre. The runtime evaluator must preserve both the formal jurisdiction and the temporary servicing arrangement rather than treating Southern California/Arizona/Nevada/New Mexico as an ordinary San Francisco-jurisdiction case.
