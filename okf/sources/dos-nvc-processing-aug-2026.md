# NVC Processing and One-Year Contact Rule

Key controls:

- NVC handles pre-processing after petition approval for cases proceeding through a U.S. embassy or consulate.
- A preference-case priority date remains a primary control on when processing and visa-number-dependent steps can advance.
- If a visa is available and NVC has not contacted the applicant, the Department directs applicants to use the NVC Public Inquiry Form.
- INA 203(g) creates a serious termination risk when an applicant fails to apply/respond within one year after notice of visa availability. Limited reinstatement may be possible within two years when the failure was for reasons beyond the applicant's control.
- The checklist therefore never treats prolonged NVC inactivity as harmless.
