# Social Security retirement/spouse claiming rules — August 2026

Authoritative source for coordination between retirement and spouse/divorced-spouse benefits.

Key controls:

- for people subject to current deemed-filing rules, filing for retirement or spouse benefits can require filing for the other benefit as well;
- SSA pays the applicable combined/higher amount rather than adding two full benefits together;
- deemed filing does not apply to survivor benefits in the same way, so survivor/retirement sequencing requires separate treatment;
- delayed retirement credits stop at age 70;
- voluntary-suspension rules can affect benefits payable on the worker's record and should not be simplified into a generic 'file and suspend' strategy.
