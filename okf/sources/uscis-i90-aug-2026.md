# USCIS Form I-90 — August 2026

Key controls:

- Lawful permanent residents use I-90 to renew or replace qualifying Permanent Resident Cards.
- Conditional permanent residents may use I-90 for certain replacement situations but must not use I-90 to remove conditions as the two-year card approaches expiration.
- Marriage-based conditional residents use the I-751 process to remove conditions; conditional immigrant investors use I-829.
- Card renewal, lost/stolen/damaged replacement, data correction and removal of conditions are separate branches.
