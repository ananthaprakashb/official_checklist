# USCIS H-1B / Form I-129 — August 2026

Key controls:

- Form I-129 is filed by the petitioning employer for H-1B and other petition-based temporary worker classifications.
- H-1B filings include classification-specific supplements and Department of Labor Labor Condition Application controls.
- When an H-1B fiscal-year registration is required, a cap-subject petition must align with a valid selected registration/beneficiary confirmation and the identity document used for registration.
- Cap-exempt, change-of-employer, extension, amendment and cap-subject cases are different branches and should not be collapsed into one checklist.
