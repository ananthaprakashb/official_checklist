# USCIS Form I-130 — August 2026

Authoritative source for family relationship immigrant petitions.

Key controls:

- Form I-130 is the family petition used by qualifying U.S. citizens or lawful permanent residents for eligible relatives.
- The relationship category affects immigrant classification and whether visa numbers are immediately available or preference-limited.
- Approval of I-130 does not itself grant permanent residence.
- After the petition stage, the beneficiary may pursue adjustment of status inside the United States when eligible or Department of State consular processing abroad.
