# Form I-693 and Form I-485 Filing

USCIS announced that applicants who are required to submit Form I-693 with Form I-485 must include it with the adjustment filing or the I-485 may be rejected. The employment-based preflight therefore treats an unresolved/absent required medical package as a filing blocker for a planned new I-485 rather than relying on older advice to automatically submit the medical later.

Always use the current I-485 and I-693 filing pages/editions immediately before filing.
