# USCIS Form I-485 — August 2026

Authoritative source for adjustment of status to lawful permanent residence.

Key controls:

- Form I-485 is the USCIS adjustment-of-status application for eligible applicants pursuing permanent residence from inside the United States.
- The underlying immigrant basis must support adjustment and any numerical visa-availability requirement must be satisfied before filing where applicable.
- Employment-based and family-based cases can have different prerequisite petition, evidence and concurrent-filing rules.
- Do not route a beneficiary residing abroad into I-485 merely because an immigrant petition is approved; consular processing is a separate path.
