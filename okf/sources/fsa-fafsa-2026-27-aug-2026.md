# 2026-27 FAFSA

Authoritative award-year source for the FAFSA covering July 1, 2026 through June 30, 2027.

Key controls:

- the 2026-27 FAFSA is available for the current award year;
- the federal FAFSA deadline is June 30, 2027;
- state and school deadlines can be much earlier and may require additional forms;
- the FAFSA is used for federal grants, work-study and loans, and is also used by states and schools for aid decisions.
