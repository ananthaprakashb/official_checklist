# U.S. Passport Services — August 2026

Authoritative routing source for U.S. passport application, renewal, replacement, correction and urgent-travel channels.

Key controls:

- applicants who are not eligible for renewal must generally apply again in person using the DS-11 route;
- a child under age 16 cannot renew with DS-82 and uses an in-person child-passport process;
- lost/stolen, materially damaged, correction/name-change and limited-validity cases have distinct replacement/correction rules;
- passport agencies/centers serve qualifying urgent travel, including international travel within 14 days or a foreign visa needed within 28 days under current State Department instructions.
