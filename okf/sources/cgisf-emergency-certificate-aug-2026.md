# CGI San Francisco Emergency Certificate Guidance — August 2026

Current official EC guidance used by the U.S. passport bundle.

Key facts:

- EC is a one-way travel document to India when a valid passport is unavailable because of loss, damage or expiry and a new passport cannot be issued immediately.
- Existing passport is cancelled when EC is issued.
- Application may be submitted in person or, subject to the page's conditions, by post directly to the Consulate rather than through the normal VFS passport flow.
- Lost/stolen cases require a police report; lost/stolen/damaged cases require Annexure F.
- The page lists EC-specific photos, address proof, affidavits/undertakings and current consular fee instructions.
