# Immigrant Visa Interview Preparation

Current controls:

- After NVC schedules an interview, each applicant must follow the assigned embassy/consulate's current pre-interview instructions.
- Each applicant and derivative must complete the immigrant-visa medical examination with an embassy-approved panel physician before interview.
- Applicants must bring required photographs and original or certified civil documents corresponding to the copies submitted to NVC.
- Police-certificate validity must be rechecked before interview; updated originals may be required.
- The DS-260 confirmation page and a qualifying passport are core interview items.
- Post-specific instructions can add courier registration, document format or employment-evidence requirements; the generic NVC checklist does not override them.
