# 20 CFR 656.21 — Supervised recruitment

Key controls used by Official Checklist:

- A Certifying Officer may require post-filing supervised recruitment for a pending PERM case or future cases.
- The employer must provide a draft advertisement to the Certifying Officer for review and approval within 30 days after notification that supervised recruitment is required.
- Recruitment media and advertisement content are controlled by the Certifying Officer; the employer must not independently publish the supervised-recruitment advertisement before approval.
- If the advertisement runs in a newspaper of general circulation, it runs for three consecutive days, one of which must be Sunday; a professional/trade/ethnic publication follows the next available published edition when directed.
- The employer submits a signed, detailed recruitment report within 30 days of the Certifying Officer's request.
- Failure to timely provide required supervised-recruitment documentation or information results in denial, subject to the regulation's discretionary good-cause extension provision.
