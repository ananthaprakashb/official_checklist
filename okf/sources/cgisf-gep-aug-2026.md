# CGI San Francisco Global Entry Program Guidance — August 2026

Official source for the India-side background-verification portion of U.S. CBP Global Entry.

Current sequence:

1. Apply for Global Entry with U.S. CBP.
2. Submit the required information through Passport Seva for background verification in India.
3. Submit the physical application, current passport copy, present-address proof and applicable fee through VFS.

The Consulate states that no firm processing timeline is defined because multiple government departments participate in the vetting process.
