# California DMV AB 60 Driver Licenses

Primary source for the California AB 60 driver-license path.

Key controls encoded by the runtime:

- AB 60 is a driver-license path for an applicant who cannot provide proof of legal presence but can satisfy California identity and residency requirements;
- AB 60 is not a REAL ID credential;
- the applicant completes the DL application, knowledge test, identity/residency review, and behind-the-wheel requirements;
- if acceptable identity/residency evidence cannot be established at the normal document check, DMV may require Secondary Review.
