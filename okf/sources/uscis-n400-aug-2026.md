# USCIS Form N-400 — August 2026

Key controls:

- N-400 contains multiple naturalization bases, including the general lawful-permanent-resident provision, marriage to a U.S. citizen and military-related provisions.
- The correct eligibility basis must be identified before applying.
- USCIS warns that a person who may already be a U.S. citizen should resolve citizenship status rather than blindly filing N-400.
- Evidence and testing exceptions can depend on the selected naturalization basis and applicant facts.
