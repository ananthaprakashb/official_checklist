# USCIS Form I-539 — August 2026

Key controls:

- Form I-539 is used by eligible nonimmigrants for certain extensions of stay or changes of status inside the United States.
- It is not the form used by a principal H-1B worker; that employer petition is Form I-129.
- A person seeking an H-4 visa abroad follows the Department of State visa process rather than filing I-539 merely for visa issuance.
- Some status categories may not use I-539, so classification must occur before documents or fees are collected.
