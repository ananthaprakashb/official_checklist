# USCIS Visa Availability and Priority Dates

Authoritative USCIS control for employment-based adjustment filing eligibility.

Key controls:

- Employment-based preference visas are numerically limited and priority-date driven.
- The priority date is generally the DOL labor-certification filing date when labor certification is required, or the immigrant-petition filing date when it is not.
- USCIS publishes which Department of State Visa Bulletin chart adjustment applicants may use for filing in a given month.
- A Department of State Dates-for-Filing date cannot be used for an I-485 filing unless USCIS has selected that chart for the relevant month.
- Even after an I-485 is properly filed, Final Action visa availability remains a separate approval gate and can retrogress.

Because the USCIS chart-selection page is time-sensitive and may change monthly, the runtime requires that selection to be explicitly verified rather than inferred from the more favorable chart.
