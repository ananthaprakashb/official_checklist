# Form I-824 — Approved-Petition Consular Routing

USCIS Form I-824 includes an action requesting USCIS to send an approved immigrant-visa petition to the National Visa Center. The exact need for Form I-824 depends on the approved petition's current routing/history and must not be assumed universally.

The NVC workflow therefore checks whether the approved employment petition has actually been forwarded to DOS/NVC. If USCIS retained the petition or consular processing was not the approved routing, the case must resolve the USCIS-to-DOS transfer before treating the NVC process as active.
