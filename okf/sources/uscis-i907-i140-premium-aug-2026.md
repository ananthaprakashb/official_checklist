# USCIS I-907 / I-140 Premium Processing — August 2026

Current Form I-907 instructions identify the I-140 classifications designated for premium processing.

- 15-day processing: E11, E12, E21 non-NIW, E31, E32, and EW3.
- 45-day processing after prerequisites are met: E13 and E21 NIW.
- Premium processing must be requested through Form I-907 for an eligible underlying petition; the beneficiary does not independently request premium processing for an employer petition.
- Premium processing does not change the substantive evidence or eligibility requirements of Form I-140.
