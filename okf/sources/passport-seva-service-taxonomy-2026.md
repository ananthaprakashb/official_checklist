# Passport Seva Application Form Service Taxonomy

Authoritative source for service classification.

Key rules used by this bundle:

- Fresh Ordinary Passport applies when the applicant has never held an Ordinary Passport, even if an Official or Diplomatic Passport is/was held.
- Re-issue applies when another Ordinary Passport is requested in lieu of an existing/previous Ordinary Passport.
- Re-issue reasons include change in particulars, validity expiry, exhaustion of pages, damaged passport, lost passport, and related replacement cases.
- Passport-related miscellaneous services include PCC, GEP background verification, Surrender Certificate and Certificate of Identity.
- Certificate of Identity is for stateless persons, including Tibetan refugees residing in India.
