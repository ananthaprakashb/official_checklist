# Immigrant Visa Interview Scheduling

The Department's current scheduling controls distinguish document readiness from visa availability:

- NVC assigns a documentarily complete date after required fees and documents are received and reviewed.
- Interview capacity is post-specific and the IV Scheduling Status Tool reports which documentarily complete months a post is generally scheduling.
- Employment-based preference interviews can only be scheduled when a visa number is available under the Visa Bulletin Final Action Dates chart.
- A priority date must be earlier than the applicable Final Action cutoff, or the category must be Current.
- Retrogression can stop interview scheduling even for a documentarily complete case.
- The IV Scheduling Status Tool is informational and does not guarantee an appointment date.
