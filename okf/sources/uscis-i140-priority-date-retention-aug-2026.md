# Employment-Based Priority-Date Retention — August 2026

USCIS recognizes priority-date retention across certain EB-1, EB-2 and EB-3 petitions when the regulatory conditions are met.

Controls used here:

- A beneficiary with an earlier approved EB-1/EB-2/EB-3 petition may be able to use that earlier priority date on a later qualifying EB-1/EB-2/EB-3 petition.
- Retention is not treated as automatic when the earlier approval was revoked or invalidated for fraud or willful misrepresentation, revocation/invalidation of the labor certification, or material error.
- The later petition must independently qualify for its requested classification; retaining a date does not transfer the earlier petition's evidence or eligibility.
- The petition's own priority-date basis remains distinct from the earlier date the beneficiary may be permitted to retain.
