# DOL Permanent Labor Certification (PERM) — August 2026

Authoritative source for the employer-side permanent labor certification stage.

Key controls used by Official Checklist:

- PERM is an employer process, not a beneficiary self-filing process.
- In many employment-based immigrant categories, the employer must obtain DOL labor certification before filing the immigrant petition with USCIS.
- DOL certifies that sufficient able, willing, qualified and available U.S. workers are not available for the job opportunity and that hiring the foreign worker will not adversely affect similarly employed U.S. workers.
- The employer must meet the current DOL eligibility and filing requirements, including employer identity and U.S. location requirements.
- Do not assume every employment-based category requires PERM; categories such as certain self-petitions or statutory exceptions follow different routes.
