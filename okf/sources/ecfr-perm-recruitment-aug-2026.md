# 20 CFR 656.17 — Recruitment

Key controls used by Official Checklist for standard professional and nonprofessional PERM cases:

- Mandatory recruitment must generally occur at least 30 days but no more than 180 days before ETA-9089 is filed.
- The State Workforce Agency job order runs for 30 days.
- Newspaper recruitment generally uses two different Sunday advertisements; a qualifying professional-journal substitution may replace one Sunday advertisement when the regulatory conditions are met.
- A professional occupation requires three additional recruitment steps from the regulatory list.
- Only one of those additional professional recruitment steps may consist solely of activity within 30 days of filing, and none may occur more than 180 days before filing.
- If the employer had qualifying layoffs in the area of intended employment during the six months before filing, it must notify and consider potentially qualified laid-off U.S. workers as required by the regulation.
