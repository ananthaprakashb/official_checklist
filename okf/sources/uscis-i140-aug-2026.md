# USCIS Form I-140 — August 2026

Authoritative source for employment-based immigrant petition classification.

Key controls:

- Form I-140 contains distinct petition types, including EB-1, EB-2, EB-3 and National Interest Waiver routes.
- A labor certification must accompany the petition when the selected immigrant category requires one.
- The petition records whether the beneficiary will pursue adjustment of status in the United States or immigrant-visa processing abroad.
- Do not infer that an approved I-140 alone makes Form I-485 immediately fileable; visa availability is a separate gate.
