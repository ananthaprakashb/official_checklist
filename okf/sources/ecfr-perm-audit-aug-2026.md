# 20 CFR 656.20 — Audit procedures

Key controls used by Official Checklist:

- A PERM application may be selected for audit based on review or randomly for quality control.
- The audit letter states the documentation to submit and sets a response date 30 days from the audit-letter date.
- Failure to timely provide the requested documentation results in denial and may eliminate the administrative-review route for that failure.
- The Certifying Officer may, in discretion, grant one extension of up to 30 days.
- Before final determination, the Certifying Officer may request supplemental information or require supervised recruitment.
