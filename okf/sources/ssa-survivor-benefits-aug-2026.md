# Social Security survivor benefits — August 2026

Authoritative source for surviving-spouse, surviving-divorced-spouse, child and dependent-parent benefits.

Key controls:

- a surviving spouse may qualify at age 60 or older, or at ages 50–59 with an SSA-qualifying disability;
- ordinary surviving-spouse cases generally require a nine-month marriage before death, with SSA exceptions including certain child-care situations;
- a surviving divorced spouse generally needs a marriage lasting at least 10 years;
- remarriage timing can affect survivor eligibility and requires the exact SSA rule for the applicant's age/disability facts;
- qualifying children and dependent parents use separate age/dependency rules;
- survivor benefits are not subject to the same deemed-filing rule as retirement/spouse benefits, so switching between survivor and retirement benefits may be possible in some cases;
- SSA currently does not accept survivor-benefit applications online; applicants must contact SSA by phone or through a Social Security office.
