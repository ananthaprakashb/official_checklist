# Social Security number & card — August 2026

Authoritative source for first SSN, replacement-card and personal-record routes.

Key controls:

- SSA's online number/card flow determines whether a request can finish online or requires an office/Card Center appointment;
- original/corrected-card cases use evidence requirements that vary by citizenship/noncitizen status and requested change;
- when SSA requires documentary evidence, originals or issuing-agency-certified copies are required rather than ordinary photocopies/notarized copies;
- noncitizen SSN eligibility depends on qualifying work, school, immigration or nonwork circumstances;
- SSA does not issue an SSN solely for obtaining a driver's license.
