# Visa Bulletin — August 2026

Authoritative August 2026 employment-based Final Action Dates and Dates for Filing.

Key controls encoded by the employment-green-card evaluator:

- `C` means current; `U` means unavailable.
- For a dated cutoff, the applicant's priority date must be **earlier than** the listed cutoff.
- Employment-based rows are chargeability-specific for China-mainland born, India, Mexico and Philippines, with a separate all-other column.
- USCIS adjustment applicants must separately verify which chart USCIS permits for the month; the Department of State bulletin does not by itself choose the USCIS filing chart.
- Final Action availability is distinct from a Dates-for-Filing document-submission/fileability gate.
