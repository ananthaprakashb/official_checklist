# Form I-765 — Pending Form I-485

For an adjustment applicant seeking employment authorization based on a pending Form I-485, the eligibility category is generally `(c)(9)`. The I-765 is a separate benefit request and is not evidence that the underlying I-485 is approvable.

Use the current Form I-765 edition, filing fee rules and filing instructions at the time of submission.
