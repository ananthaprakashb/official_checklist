# NVC Fees and CEAC Access

Current controls:

- After receiving the NVC Welcome Letter, applicants use CEAC and the case credentials supplied by NVC.
- The immigrant-visa application processing fee must be paid for each intending immigrant and is currently $345 per person for employment-based immigrant-visa processing.
- The Affidavit of Support review fee is currently $120 when the case requires that service; a small number of cases do not require it.
- NVC states that Form DS-260 is not accessible until the required fee has been processed as paid.
- Fee amounts and payment instructions are freshness-sensitive and must be rechecked before payment.
