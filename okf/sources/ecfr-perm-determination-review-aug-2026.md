# 20 CFR 656.24 / 656.26 — Determination and review

Key controls used by Official Checklist:

- The Certifying Officer grants or denies the labor certification based on compliance with Part 656, availability of qualified U.S. workers, and effect on U.S. wages and working conditions.
- A denial states the reasons and explains review procedures.
- A request for review of a denial or revocation must be sent within 30 days of the determination to the Certifying Officer, identify the determination, state the grounds, and include the Final Determination.
- Failure to request timely review makes the denial final under the regulation.
- A new application for the same occupation and same foreign worker cannot be filed while a request for review is pending before BALCA.
- Reconsideration is also subject to the regulatory 30-day timing and evidentiary limits.
