# Social Security retirement earnings test — 2026

Authoritative source for current-year work/earnings effects on retirement, family and survivor benefits before full retirement age.

Key controls for 2026:

- under full retirement age for the entire year: $24,480 annual limit, with $1 in benefits withheld for each $2 above the limit;
- in the year full retirement age is reached: $65,160 limit for earnings before the full-retirement-age month, with $1 withheld for each $3 above the limit;
- beginning with the month full retirement age is reached, the earnings test no longer reduces benefits;
- SSA has a special monthly rule for some people who retire mid-year;
- survivor-benefit earnings-test calculations use the retirement full retirement age for this purpose.
