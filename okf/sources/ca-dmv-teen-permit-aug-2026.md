# California DMV Learner Permit and Provisional License

Primary source for California instruction permits and under-18 licensing.

Key controls encoded by the runtime:

- an under-18 instruction-permit applicant must be at least 15 1/2 and complete or be enrolled in qualifying driver education as required by DMV;
- the provisional-license route is distinct from the permit route;
- before the under-18 drive test, DMV requires the provisional permit to have been held for at least six months plus the applicable driver-education/training/practice milestones;
- driving-test appointments and financial-responsibility evidence are separate downstream requirements.
