# USCIS Form I-290B — August 2026

Form I-290B is the USCIS appeal/motion vehicle for benefit types within its jurisdiction, including many adverse employment-petition decisions.

Controls used here:

- The actual I-140 decision notice controls whether an appeal or motion is available and where/how it must be filed.
- Only the petitioner or applicant with standing, or the authorized representative for that party, may generally file; a beneficiary cannot assume independent appeal standing for an employer-filed petition.
- The engine does not guess a denial deadline. It requires the user to read the decision notice and verify the stated filing period and jurisdiction.
- A new petition, appeal, motion to reopen, and motion to reconsider are distinct strategies and are not treated as interchangeable.
