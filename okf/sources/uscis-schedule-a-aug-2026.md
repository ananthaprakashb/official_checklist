# USCIS Schedule A — August 2026

USCIS applies the Department of Labor Schedule A framework when adjudicating qualifying Form I-140 petitions.

- Group I includes registered nurses and physical therapists.
- Group II includes beneficiaries with exceptional ability in the sciences or arts; the regulatory framework separately addresses performing arts within Group II.
- Schedule A employers submit the labor-certification package directly to USCIS rather than first obtaining an individualized PERM certification from DOL.
- A Schedule A designation does not replace the underlying EB-2 or EB-3 immigrant-classification requirements.
