# Visa Bulletin — September 2026

Current authoritative immigrant-visa availability source.

Key controls:

- The bulletin publishes both Final Action Dates and Dates for Filing for numerically limited family- and employment-based categories.
- A priority date must be earlier than the applicable cutoff when the category is not current.
- USCIS separately announces which chart adjustment-of-status applicants may use for filing in a given month.
- Dates can advance, retrogress or become unavailable, so visa-availability rules are freshness-critical and must never be cached indefinitely.
