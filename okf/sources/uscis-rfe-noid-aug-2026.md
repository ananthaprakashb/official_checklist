# USCIS RFE / NOID — August 2026

Official Checklist treats the actual USCIS notice as controlling.

- An RFE or NOID identifies the evidentiary issue and the response deadline.
- The engine must not invent a new response date from a generic processing estimate.
- The regulatory maximum response period for an RFE is 12 weeks and for a NOID is 30 days, but the actual notice may provide a shorter response period.
- USCIS guidance provides next-business-day treatment when a mailed RFE/NOID response deadline falls on a Saturday, Sunday, or federal holiday.
- Missing a notice deadline or responding without addressing every stated ground is a blocker requiring immediate authoritative/legal review.
