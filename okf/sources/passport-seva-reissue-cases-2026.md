# Passport Seva Re-issue Case Router

Authoritative list of selectable re-issue case families:

- Exhaustion of Pages;
- Validity Due to Expire;
- Validity Expired;
- Renewal of Short Validity Passport (SVP);
- Lost/Stolen Passport;
- Damaged Passport;
- Change in Existing Personal Particulars;
- None of the Above.

This source prevents the OKF graph from collapsing SVP renewal or stolen-passport cases into an incomplete generic renewal branch.
