# USCIS Form I-485 Instructions — Employment-Based Adjustment

Authoritative filing instructions used by the detailed employment-based adjustment preflight.

Key controls:

- Adjustment of status is a U.S.-based permanent-residence process and requires the applicant to satisfy the applicable INA 245 eligibility framework.
- Employment-based preference applicants remain subject to visa-number availability and the applicable filing chart selected by USCIS for the month of filing.
- The underlying immigrant petition must support the adjustment filing; concurrent filing is category- and visa-availability-dependent.
- Filing eligibility and final approval eligibility are separate because Final Action availability can change after filing.
- Admissibility, adjustment bars, inspection/admission/parole, status history and other individualized facts can independently prevent adjustment even when a visa number is available.
