# PERM Notice of Filing — August 2026

Key controls used by Official Checklist:

- Notice is provided to the bargaining representative when one exists for the occupational classification at the location of intended employment.
- If there is no bargaining representative, the employer posts notice at the facility/location of employment for 10 consecutive business days in a conspicuous place.
- The employer also uses applicable in-house media, electronic or printed, under the employer's normal procedures for recruitment for similar positions.
- For a posted Notice of Filing, the 10 consecutive business days must fall within the 30-to-180-day prefiling window, and the posting must end at least 30 days before ETA-9089 is filed.
- The notice must contain the required job and wage information and DOL notice language.
