# 20 CFR 656.30 — Certification validity

Key controls used by Official Checklist:

- The approved PERM filing date may be used as the employment-based priority date by DHS and the Department of State.
- A certified permanent labor certification granted on or after July 16, 2007 expires if it is not filed in support of Form I-140 within 180 calendar days of DOL certification.
- A certification involving a specific job offer is valid only for the particular job opportunity, named foreign worker, and area of intended employment stated on ETA-9089, subject to the regulation.
- A certification may later be revoked or invalidated under the applicable regulatory procedures, including for fraud or willful material misrepresentation.
