# VFS USA Surrender of Indian Passport Guidance

Current VFS guidance for surrendering an Indian passport after acquisition of foreign nationality.

Key facts:

- A former Indian citizen who has acquired another country's nationality must not continue to use an Indian passport.
- Government online application is completed first.
- Online photograph and signature upload is mandatory under the current U.S. VFS process.
- Supporting documents are prepared from the applicable surrender checklist.
- Submission is completed through VFS according to jurisdiction by the available postal/courier or appointment route.
