# After Interview / INA 221(g)

Key controls:

- A consular officer may refuse an immigrant-visa application under INA 221(g) when required information or documentation is missing or when additional processing is required.
- The actual refusal sheet/instructions control what must be submitted and where.
- Some 221(g) cases enter administrative processing; duration is case-specific and must not be predicted by the checklist.
- A 221(g) refusal is not modeled as a permanent denial without the actual consular disposition.
- Applicants should not make irreversible travel/employment/property commitments until the immigrant visa has actually been issued.
