# Passport Seva Passport and Travel Document Types

Passport Seva identifies the principal travel documents issued under the Passports Act framework as:

- Ordinary Passport;
- Diplomatic Passport;
- Official Passport;
- Emergency Certificate;
- Certificate of Identity.

This source is used to keep travel-document classification distinct from passport-related miscellaneous services such as PCC, GEP and Surrender Certificate.
