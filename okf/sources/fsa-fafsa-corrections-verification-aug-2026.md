# FAFSA Submission Summary, corrections and verification

Key controls:

- after processing, the FAFSA Submission Summary identifies eligibility information, FAFSA answers, school information and next steps;
- online corrections can cover items such as missing signatures/consent, typos, contact information and adding/removing schools;
- contributors may correct only their own sections while the student can start/submit corrections for the FAFSA;
- amended-tax-return or materially changed financial circumstances may require school financial-aid-office handling rather than a normal online correction;
- verification is performed by schools to confirm FAFSA information; selection for verification is not itself evidence that the student did anything wrong.
