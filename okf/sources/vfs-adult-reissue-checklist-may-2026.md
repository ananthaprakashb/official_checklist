# VFS Adult Passport Re-issue Checklist — May 2026

Workflow-specific checklist for adult re-issue in lieu of an existing passport. It explicitly requires correct jurisdiction, `Passport Re-issue`, Government/VFS ARN alignment, mandatory online photo/signature upload, current passport, Annexure E, appearance/signature affidavit, U.S. status evidence, U.S. address proof, and branch-specific supporting documents.

It also states that a submitted online application cannot be edited; errors require a new online application.
