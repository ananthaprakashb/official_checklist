# Social Security retirement benefits — August 2026

Authoritative source for retirement-benefit eligibility, application timing and the online retirement application.

Key controls:

- retirement benefits can typically begin at age 62 for a worker with sufficient Social Security-covered work history;
- SSA describes 10 years or more of covered work as the typical retirement eligibility threshold, but the applicant's SSA record controls actual insured status;
- a retirement application can generally be filed up to four months before the requested benefit-start month;
- retirement benefits may be claimed while still working, but earnings before full retirement age can affect current payments;
- retirement claiming age changes the monthly amount, and this workflow must not choose a claiming age on the applicant's behalf.
