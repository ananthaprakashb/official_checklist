# Social Security payments outside the United States — August 2026

Authoritative source for retirement/family/survivor payment and application issues involving residence or work outside the United States.

Key controls:

- payment eligibility abroad depends on citizenship, country of residence, benefit type and other statutory conditions;
- SSA provides a Payments Abroad Screening Tool and country-specific rules rather than a single universal rule;
- international Social Security agreements can sometimes combine covered work credits for eligibility but do not transfer the foreign credits into the U.S. record;
- people living abroad may use SSA international/Federal Benefits Unit channels for services that are not available through ordinary domestic online workflows;
- work outside the United States can trigger rules different from the ordinary domestic annual earnings test.
