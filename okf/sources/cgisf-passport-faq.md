# CGI San Francisco Passport FAQ

Official consular FAQ used for service classification and the San Francisco re-issue workflow.

Key facts currently relied on by this bundle:

- A **fresh passport** is for a first passport application.
- A **re-issue** is used when another passport is requested in lieu of an existing passport, including change in particulars, expiry/due-to-expire, pages exhausted, damaged passport, or lost passport.
- The re-issue flow directs applicants to VFS Global and the Government of India passport application flow.
