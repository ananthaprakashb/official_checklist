# VFS USA Police Clearance Certificate Guidance

Current VFS guidance for PCC applications by Indian nationals in the United States.

Key facts:

- PCC is issued to Indian passport holders for purposes such as residential status, employment, long-term visa or immigration.
- It is not issued for persons going abroad on a tourist visa.
- Government online PCC application is completed first.
- Supporting documents are prepared from the applicable checklist.
- Applicants then register with VFS and use the submission/appointment path applicable to their jurisdiction.
