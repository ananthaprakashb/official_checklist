# INA 245(k) — Employment-Based Adjustment Exemption

USCIS Policy Manual authority for the limited employment-based exemption from specified adjustment bars.

Key controls used by Official Checklist:

- INA 245(k) is not a general forgiveness rule; it applies only to qualifying employment-based applicants and specified INA 245(c) bars.
- The analysis is tied to violations after the applicant's most recent lawful admission and includes failure to maintain status, unauthorized employment and other status violations.
- The statutory aggregate threshold is no more than 180 days for the covered violations.
- Eligibility is category- and fact-specific; derivatives may require their own analysis.
- A recorded violation over the statutory threshold blocks a standard 245(k)-based preflight, while uncertain or mixed history requires authoritative review rather than arithmetic guesses.
