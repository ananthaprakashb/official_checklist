# Employment-Based Fourth Preference (EB-4)

EB-4 is an employment-based preference family for qualifying special immigrants, but it is **not** the ordinary Form I-140 / PERM path.

Current USCIS Form I-360 instructions identify multiple special-immigrant classifications that may fall in EB-4. Category-specific eligibility and petition rules control. The employment-green-card router therefore identifies EB-4 and sends it to a dedicated petition path instead of silently assigning Form I-140.

Visa-number availability remains subject to the EB-4 Visa Bulletin row.
