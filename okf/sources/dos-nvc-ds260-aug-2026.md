# NVC / DS-260 Immigrant Visa Process — August 2026

Key controls:

- After USCIS approves an immigrant petition destined for consular processing, the petition is generally transferred to the National Visa Center.
- NVC collects required fees and documents and holds preference cases until immigrant-visa processing can proceed.
- Each qualified immigrating family member completes Form DS-260 when instructed and must bring the DS-260 confirmation page to interview.
- NVC processing includes petitioner financial-support evidence where applicable, applicant civil documents and later interview preparation.
- Original or certified civil documents must be brought to the immigrant visa interview even when scans were uploaded to CEAC.
