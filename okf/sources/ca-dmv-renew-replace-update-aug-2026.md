# California DMV Renew, Replace, and Update DL/ID

Primary source family for renewing, replacing, and updating California DL/ID records.

Key controls encoded by the runtime:

- online renewal eligibility differs from office-required renewal; the applicant's DMV renewal notice controls whether an office visit/testing is required;
- drivers age 70 or older must renew in person, while the knowledge-test requirement is not automatic for every 70+ renewal;
- lost/stolen/damaged noncommercial driver licenses may be eligible for online replacement, while ID-card replacement uses the office/application route described by DMV;
- a legal name change must be updated with the Social Security Administration before DMV verifies and issues the updated DL/ID;
- an address change must be reported to DMV, but it does not automatically cause DMV to issue a replacement physical card.
