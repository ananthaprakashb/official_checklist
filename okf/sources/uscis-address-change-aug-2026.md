# USCIS Change of Address — August 2026

Key controls:

- Most noncitizens in the United States must report a change of address to USCIS within 10 days of moving, subject to stated exceptions.
- Updating an address with USPS does not update the USCIS address.
- A pending USCIS case should be associated with the address update so case correspondence is routed correctly.
- Beneficiaries in Department of State consular processing may also need to update NVC/DOS separately.
- Sponsors who filed an affidavit of support may have a separate Form I-865 address-reporting duty.
