# USCIS Form I-765 — August 2026

Key controls:

- Form I-765 has many eligibility categories; the correct category is a primary routing fact, not a clerical detail.
- H-4 spouses seeking EAD under category (c)(26) must document current H-4 status/extension and the qualifying H-1B spouse relationship.
- The (c)(26) basis requires either an approved Form I-140 for the H-1B principal or a qualifying H-1B extension under AC21 sections 106(a) and (b).
- Initial, renewal and replacement EAD requests can have different evidence and timing rules.
