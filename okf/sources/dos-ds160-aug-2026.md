# DS-160 Nonimmigrant Visa Application — August 2026

Key controls:

- Electronically submitting DS-160 is only the first step in a nonimmigrant visa application.
- The applicant must follow the U.S. Embassy or Consulate's current instructions for interview eligibility and appointment scheduling.
- Petition-based temporary workers should have their Form I-129 petition information available when completing DS-160.
- Students and exchange visitors need their SEVIS/I-20 or DS-2019 information.
- Incorrect or incomplete DS-160 data can require correction and rescheduling.
