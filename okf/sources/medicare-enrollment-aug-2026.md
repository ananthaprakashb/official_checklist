# Medicare enrollment — August 2026

Authoritative source for Medicare Part A/Part B enrollment timing and forms.

Key controls:

- age-based Initial Enrollment generally spans the 7-month period starting 3 months before the month a person turns 65 and ending 3 months after;
- Part B and premium-Part A can only be added during an applicable enrollment period;
- the standard current-employment group-coverage Special Enrollment Period generally extends through 8 months after employment or qualifying group health coverage ends, whichever happens first;
- COBRA and retiree coverage do not by themselves count as current-employment group health plan coverage for that standard Part B SEP;
- current forms include CMS-40B for Part B, CMS-L564 for employment information, and CMS-10797 for qualifying exceptional-condition Special Enrollment Periods.
