# Form I-131 — Advance Parole with Pending Adjustment

Advance Parole is a separate travel-document request that may be available to a pending adjustment applicant. International travel while Form I-485 is pending can have abandonment consequences unless the applicant has the required advance-parole document or qualifies for a recognized exception.

Official Checklist therefore treats travel as a separate preflight and never interprets a pending I-131 as permission to depart.
