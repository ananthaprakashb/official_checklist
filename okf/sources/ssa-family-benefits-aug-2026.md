# Social Security family benefits — August 2026

Authoritative source for spouse, divorced-spouse and child benefits on a living worker's Retirement or Disability record.

Key controls:

- a spouse may qualify at age 62 or older, or at a younger age while caring for an entitled child age 15 or younger or a child with a qualifying disability;
- the ordinary spouse marriage-duration rule is generally one year, subject to SSA exceptions;
- a divorced spouse generally needs a marriage lasting at least 10 years and must meet the applicable age/marital-status conditions;
- if an eligible former spouse has not filed for retirement, independently entitled divorced-spouse rules can involve a two-year divorce-duration requirement;
- children have separate age, school and disability-before-age-22 rules;
- family benefits are coordinated with benefits on the applicant's own work record and are subject to SSA's family-maximum and claiming rules.
