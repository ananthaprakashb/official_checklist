# CBP I-94 Retrieval and Correction — August 2026

Key controls:

- Travelers can retrieve the most recent electronic I-94 and travel history through the CBP I-94 service.
- If an I-94 cannot be found, the traveler should first retry using biographic and travel-document data exactly as used at admission.
- CBP Deferred Inspection Sites can review and, when appropriate, correct errors made at the port of entry such as improper nonimmigrant classification, biographic data or period of admission.
- Deferred Inspection corrects CBP entry errors; it is not the process for requesting an extension of stay or change of status.
- If the incorrect I-94 was issued by USCIS rather than CBP at entry, the correction path is with USCIS.
