# USCIS Form I-131 — August 2026

Key controls:

- I-131 covers distinct travel-document families including reentry permits, refugee travel documents and advance parole.
- Advance parole does not guarantee admission; the admission/parole decision occurs at the port of entry.
- Travel while adjustment of status is pending can affect the adjustment application, subject to category-specific exceptions described by USCIS.
- Never present all I-131 uses as one interchangeable checklist; the requested travel document must be classified first.
