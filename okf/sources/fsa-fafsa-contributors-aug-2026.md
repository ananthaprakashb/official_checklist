# FAFSA student and contributor steps

Authoritative source for StudentAid.gov account, contributor and dependency-routing requirements.

Key controls:

- the student needs a StudentAid.gov account to complete the FAFSA online;
- every required contributor needs their own StudentAid.gov account and must complete/sign their own section;
- required contributors provide consent and approval for federal tax information transfer even if they did not file a U.S. tax return;
- a contributor other than the student can create an account without an SSN when applicable;
- being a FAFSA contributor does not by itself make the contributor responsible for paying the student's education costs;
- dependent students should use the FAFSA's contributor logic / Who's My FAFSA Parent tool rather than choosing a parent based only on where the student lives.
