# Passport Seva Appeal Guidance

Authoritative guidance for appeals against adverse passport actions.

An applicant may file an appeal when an adverse action such as rejection/refusal of an application or impounding/revocation of a passport has been taken. The online flow requires the affected application/file, appeal details, payment of the appeal fee and supporting documents such as the speaking order or adverse-action notice.
