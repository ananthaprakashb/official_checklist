# Form I-485 Supplement J — Employment-Based Adjustment

Current USCIS controls used by this bundle:

- Supplement J confirms a bona fide job offer underlying an employment-based I-140 when required, or requests INA 204(j) job portability.
- A portability request generally requires the Form I-485 to have been properly filed and pending for 180 days or more, with a qualifying new permanent job offer in the same or similar occupational classification.
- USCIS instructions state that applicants seeking or granted EB-1A extraordinary-ability classification or an EB-2 National Interest Waiver do not file Supplement J merely to confirm a job offer or request 204(j) portability because those categories are not tied to the job-offer requirement in the same way.
- Whether Supplement J is needed at initial I-485 filing depends on the petition/final filing posture; do not add it universally to every employment-based package.
