# PERM Recruitment and Filing Timeline

For the standard 20 CFR 656.17 route:

## Prevailing wage

- Obtain a valid NPWC prevailing wage determination before ETA-9089 filing.
- Either ETA-9089 filing or the beginning of required recruitment must occur while the PWD is valid.
- The offered wage may not be below the applicable prevailing wage.

## Professional occupations

- 30-day State Workforce Agency job order.
- Two different Sunday advertisements in the appropriate newspaper, subject to regulatory rural-area and professional-journal alternatives.
- Three additional recruitment steps from the methods permitted by 20 CFR 656.17(e)(1)(ii).
- Only one additional professional recruitment step may consist solely of activity within the 30 days immediately before ETA-9089 filing.
- No required recruitment step may be older than 180 days at filing.

## Nonprofessional occupations

- 30-day State Workforce Agency job order.
- Two different Sunday advertisements, subject to the regulatory rural-area alternative.
- The mandatory recruitment steps must fall in the 30-to-180-day prefiling period.

## Notice of Filing

- If a bargaining representative exists, provide the notice through that route.
- Otherwise post for 10 consecutive business days in a conspicuous location and use applicable in-house media under the employer's normal practice.
- For a posted notice, the posting must be within the 30-to-180-day prefiling period and end at least 30 days before filing.

## Layoffs and recruitment record

- Qualifying employer layoffs in the area of intended employment during the six months before filing trigger the laid-off-worker notification/consideration rule.
- Maintain a recruitment report and supporting evidence sufficient for an audit.
- Retain ETA-9089 and supporting documentation for five years from filing.
