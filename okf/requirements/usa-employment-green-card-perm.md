# PERM Stage Requirements

Run only after the category router resolves an ordinary DOL PERM-required case.

## Core sequence

1. Employer identifies the permanent full-time job opportunity and actual minimum requirements.
2. Employer obtains a valid Prevailing Wage Determination from DOL.
3. Employer completes the occupation-specific pre-filing recruitment and Notice of Filing requirements.
4. Employer files ETA Form 9089 through the current DOL PERM process.
5. If certified, preserve the certification and priority-date data for the downstream immigrant petition.
6. Submit the certified labor certification with the qualifying Form I-140 within the certification's validity period; DOL states a certified PERM is valid for 180 days for this purpose.

## Guardrails

- The employer, not the employee, files the PERM application.
- Do not force EB-1A, EB-1B, EB-1C, EB-2 NIW, EB-4 or EB-5 into ordinary PERM.
- Do not route Schedule A through ordinary PERM; it has a separate labor-certification procedure.
- A denied or expired PERM is not a valid downstream I-140 prerequisite.
