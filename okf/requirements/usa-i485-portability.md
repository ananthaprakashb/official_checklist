# Supplement J / INA 204(j) Portability

For qualifying job-offer-based EB-1, EB-2 and EB-3 adjustment cases:

- Supplement J may be required to confirm the continuing bona fide job offer;
- INA 204(j) portability generally requires the I-485 to have been pending 180 days or more;
- the new permanent job offer must be in the same or a similar occupational classification;
- Supplement J is used to request the portability determination.

EB-1A extraordinary-ability and EB-2 NIW cases are not job-offer-based and do not use Supplement J merely to request 204(j) portability. A change in employer or work plan for those classifications must be evaluated under the underlying classification rules instead.
