# I-140 Category Evidence

## Cross-category gates

- Select exactly one I-140 classification.
- Establish eligibility under that classification as of the required filing/priority-date point; do not rely on facts that arise only after filing when the regulation requires earlier qualification.
- Employer-filed classifications requiring a job offer must document the qualifying permanent job offer and continuing ability to pay the proffered wage.
- PERM-backed petitions must include the certified labor certification while it is valid for the new petition and must align the offered job and beneficiary qualifications with the labor certification.
- Schedule A must use the direct-to-USCIS Schedule A procedure rather than a DOL-certified PERM.

## Category-specific evidence

### EB-1A / E11

Show either the qualifying one-time major internationally recognized award or the required regulatory evidence framework, followed by the overall merits analysis, and show intent to continue work in the area of extraordinary ability. No job offer or labor certification is required.

### EB-1B / E12

Show international recognition as outstanding, at least three years of qualifying teaching/research experience, and the required permanent/tenure-track research or teaching offer from a qualifying U.S. employer. Private research employers must satisfy the additional employer requirements in the I-140 instructions.

### EB-1C / E13

Show the qualifying multinational relationship, required period of qualifying managerial/executive employment abroad, the U.S. petitioner's required period of doing business, and a primarily managerial/executive U.S. position.

### EB-2 regular / E21 non-NIW

Show the underlying advanced-degree-professional or exceptional-ability eligibility and the required employer/labor-certification route. The labor-certified position must support the requested EB-2 classification, and the beneficiary must satisfy the certified requirements.

### EB-2 NIW / E21 NIW

First establish EB-2 advanced-degree or exceptional-ability eligibility, then separately establish the national-interest-waiver standard. Because the NIW merits analysis is fact-intensive, the engine returns authoritative confirmation unless the category and evidence framework are clearly identified.

### EB-3 Skilled / E31

Show at least two years of training or experience when required by the classification and establish every education/training/experience requirement in the labor certification.

### EB-3 Professional / E32

Show a U.S. bachelor's degree or foreign equivalent and that a bachelor's degree is normally required for entry into the occupation, plus the labor-certification requirements.

### EB-3 Other Worker / EW3

Show the beneficiary satisfies the education/training/experience requirements of the certified labor certification.

### Schedule A

Identify the Schedule A group and underlying EB-2 or EB-3 classification. Preserve the Schedule A prevailing-wage/Notice-of-Filing and occupation-specific evidence rather than treating the case as ordinary PERM.
