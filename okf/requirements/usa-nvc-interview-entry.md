# Interview → Visa → U.S. Admission Requirements

Before interview:

- Follow the actual NVC appointment letter and the assigned embassy/consulate's current instructions.
- Confirm Final Action visa availability; DQ alone does not create a visa number.
- Complete the medical with the post-authorized panel physician for every applicant.
- Recheck police certificates and other civil documents for interview validity.
- Bring the DS-260 confirmation page, passport, photographs and originals/certified civil documents required by the post.
- For employer/job-offer categories, preserve the qualifying permanent-employment basis and follow the post's current employer-letter/evidence instructions.
- For EB-1A/NIW, preserve the qualifying intent to continue the work/endeavor rather than inventing a job-offer requirement.

After interview:

- Follow the actual consular instructions for any INA 221(g) refusal or administrative processing. Do not predict completion time.
- If a visa is issued, check the printed visa information and its expiration date before travel.
- Do not plan U.S. admission after the immigrant visa expires.
- Pay the USCIS Immigrant Fee before travel unless exempt when possible. Nonpayment does not itself invalidate the immigrant visa, but USCIS will not produce the Green Card until the fee is paid.
- Lawful permanent resident status is acquired through admission by CBP on the immigrant visa, not merely by NVC document completion or visa issuance.
