# Minor Re-issue Documents — USA

Apply these controls in addition to the common and reason-specific re-issue requirements.

## Minor signature/consent rules

- minor applicant signs/writes their name where prescribed;
- minor below age 5 may use the left thumb impression;
- both parents sign the application in the prescribed places;
- Annexure D is required and signed/notarized as prescribed by the current checklist;
- Annexure C is conditionally required when one parent has not given consent;
- if the minor's signature changed, record that fact and complete the corresponding appearance/signature affidavit branch.

## Lost/damaged minor branch

When the minor passport is lost or damaged, combine these parental/Annexure requirements with the corresponding lost/damaged checklist and Annexure F requirements.

## Guardrail

Do not reuse the adult checklist for a minor application. An adult/minor checklist mismatch is `NOT_READY`.
