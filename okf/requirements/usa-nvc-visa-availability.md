# NVC Visa Availability Gates

Keep two numerical-availability questions separate:

1. **NVC pre-processing / document collection** — use the Department of State Dates for Filing framework and the actual NVC notice/instructions for the case. A case should not start fee/document work merely because a later Final Action date might someday become current.
2. **Interview / visa issuance availability** — employment-based preference interviews require a visa number under the **Final Action Dates** chart. The priority date must be earlier than the cutoff or the category must be Current.

A documentarily complete case can remain valid while Final Action is unavailable. In that state the correct action is to preserve the case, monitor the Visa Bulletin and post scheduling status, and respond to NVC notices.

Retrogression does not erase documentarily complete status, but it can stop interview scheduling or visa issuance.

The one-year NVC contact/apply requirement under INA 203(g) is a separate preservation gate and must not be ignored during a long visa-number wait.
