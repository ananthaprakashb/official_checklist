# Employment-Based I-485 Filing Package

Core package:

- current Form I-485 edition/instructions and correct filing location;
- evidence of the underlying employment-based immigrant basis/petition;
- priority-date and visa-availability evidence for the month of filing;
- identity, civil, immigration-status and admission/parole evidence required by the current instructions;
- required Form I-693 medical package submitted with the I-485 under current USCIS filing policy;
- Form I-485 Supplement J when the job-offer-based filing posture requires it.

Optional/separate requests:

- Form I-765 under the correct pending-adjustment category when EAD is desired;
- Form I-131 when Advance Parole is desired.

Derivative spouse/children require their own I-485 eligibility and evidence; the principal's READY result does not automatically establish derivative eligibility.
