# NVC CEAC / DS-260 Requirements

For each intending immigrant:

- Use the NVC case number / Invoice ID and the current CEAC instructions supplied in the Welcome Letter.
- Pay the current immigrant-visa application processing fee. The bundled August 2026 fee is $345 per intending immigrant.
- Do not treat a payment submitted but still processing as a completed fee when DS-260 access is not yet available.
- Complete and submit Form DS-260 for every qualified principal/derivative applicant and preserve each confirmation page for interview.
- Submit civil-document scans/copies exactly as NVC instructs and check the Department's country-specific Reciprocity and Civil Documents schedule.
- Do not send original passports or irreplaceable original civil documents to NVC unless explicitly instructed; originals/certified copies are generally carried to interview.

Employment-based financial-support handling:

- Do not require Form I-864 for every employment case.
- Confirm the limited employment-based I-864 rule when a U.S. citizen/LPR relative filed the employment petition or has a significant ownership interest in the petitioning entity.
- If that exception applies, follow NVC's current Affidavit-of-Support fee and document requirements.

Documentarily complete status is an NVC determination after its review; the checklist must not self-declare DQ merely because files were uploaded.
