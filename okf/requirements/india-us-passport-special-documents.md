# Indian Diplomatic, Official and Identity Certificate Requirements — USA

This node describes only stable Government classification/document gates. It does not assume that an India-based special-document workflow is directly usable by a U.S.-resident applicant.

## Diplomatic / Official passport core evidence

Depending on the Government category, core evidence includes:

- Government/official identity evidence;
- Head of Office certificate;
- request/forwarding letter from the authorized forwarding officer;
- applicable political/PMO or other Government clearance;
- existing passport or safe-custody evidence when applicable;
- any mission-specific forms and submission instructions.

## Identity Certificate

Certificate of Identity is a special travel document for persons who satisfy the Government's stateless-person/refugee eligibility rules. It must not be classified as an Ordinary Passport simply because the applicant needs a travel document.

## U.S. handling gate

Before any of these branches can return READY, verify a current procedure from the Indian Mission/Post or competent Government authority that explicitly covers the applicant's category and U.S. location. Until then, return `NEEDS_AUTHORITATIVE_CONFIRMATION`.
