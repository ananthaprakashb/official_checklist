# Indian Emergency Certificate Requirements — San Francisco Serviced Cases

Run only after [Emergency travel routing](../decisions/india-us-passport-emergency-travel-route.md) resolves `emergency_certificate` and the applicant is within the San Francisco-serviced route.

## Required application package

- completed online Emergency Certificate application form;
- two current white-background color photographs, 2 x 2 inches (51 x 51 mm);
- applicable EC and ICWF payments under the current Consulate instructions;
- completed EC Affidavit;
- self-attested copies of passport first/last/visa pages and original passport when available;
- police report when the passport is lost, stolen or misplaced, identifying the applicant and referring to the lost passport;
- Annexure F for lost, stolen or damaged passport cases;
- self-attested U.S. address proof; if unavailable, the applicable Annexure V affidavit;
- undertaking where the passport is withheld by USCIS / Department of Homeland Security;
- applicant-written statement describing entry to the U.S., current status, reason for returning to India and proof of travel;
- any other affidavit/annexure required by the current EC application page for the applicant's facts.

## Submission-specific controls

- EC is submitted directly to the Consulate under the current EC instructions rather than through the normal VFS passport-reissue package.
- In-person or postal submission may be available subject to the current page conditions.
- Postal applications for people in detention require prior Consulate approval; do not represent a detained applicant as postal-ready without that approval.

## Verification gate

Issuance depends on confirmation of passport particulars and Indian nationality. If the Consulate cannot confirm these directly, the case depends on clearance from authorities in India; return `NEEDS_AUTHORITATIVE_CONFIRMATION` for timing rather than promising same-day/next-day issuance.
