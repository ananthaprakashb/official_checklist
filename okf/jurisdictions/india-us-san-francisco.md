# Indian Consular Jurisdiction — San Francisco

Current official guidance identifies the San Francisco jurisdiction for passport-related processing as covering **Northern and Central California, Colorado, Hawaii, Utah, Wyoming, and Guam**.

## Guardrail

Do not infer the California jurisdiction boundary from a city name when the applicant may be near a consular boundary. The application must use the Indian Mission/consular jurisdiction required by the current official process.
