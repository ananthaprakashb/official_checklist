# Indian Emergency Certificate Fee — San Francisco

Current CGI San Francisco EC page publishes:

- EC fee: **$15.00**;
- Indian Community Welfare Fund contribution: **$2.00**;
- total: **$17.00**.

The current page instructs applicants to pay these as two separate money orders or cashier's checks to the named Consulate/ICWF payees. Personal checks, credit/debit cards and other banking instruments are not accepted for this EC payment path.
