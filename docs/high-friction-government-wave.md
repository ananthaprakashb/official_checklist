# High-Friction Government Process Wave — August 2026

This wave expands Official Checklist into government processes that affect large numbers of households and commonly fail because the applicant starts with the wrong form, agency, enrollment period, or submission channel.

## Activated live classifiers

- **U.S. Passport Services** — first adult, child, renewal, lost/stolen, damaged, correction, limited-validity and urgent travel.
- **Social Security Number & Card** — first number, replacement, name/record correction, citizenship/immigration update, child card and identity-theft routing.
- **IRS ITIN** — SSN eligibility, new/renew W-7, return/exception route, identity evidence and submission/authentication method.
- **Medicare Enrollment & Part B Timing** — Initial Enrollment, current-employment Special Enrollment, exceptional SEP, General Enrollment and plan-change routing.
- **Family-Based Green Card** — petitioner/relationship classification, immediate-relative vs family-preference category, I-130 and I-485/NVC handoff.

## Selection criteria

These processes scored highly for population reach, wrong-branch risk, consequences of delay, multiple forms/portals, changing instructions, and the availability of authoritative federal sources that support deterministic preflight rules.

## Next candidates

The next onboarding group should prioritize REAL ID/driver licensing, Social Security retirement/survivor benefits, FAFSA, unemployment/Paid Family Leave, vital records, Global Entry/TSA PreCheck, FEMA disaster assistance, VA benefits, EIN/tax identity workflows, and state property/tax processes.

Every activated process must continue to meet the repository's existing source-freshness, official-link, questionnaire, evaluator, TypeScript and production-build gates.
